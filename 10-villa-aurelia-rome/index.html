The Hotel

Built for painters in 1921, kept for those who love Rome slowly

Villa Aurelia began life as a row of artists' studios, their north-facing windows chosen for the soft light painters crave. Today those same windows frame our rooms, and the courtyard where sculptors once worked is our garden of lemon trees and climbing jasmine.

We are deliberately small. Twenty-eight rooms means our team knows your coffee order by the second morning, your favourite table by the third, and the trattoria you will want to return to long after you have gone home.

Giulia Ferrante, Owner

Experiences

The eternal city, unhurried

Our team grew up between these piazzas. We will get you into the Sistine Chapel before the doors open and out to a vineyard in the Castelli Romani by lunchtime.

  1. 01

    Vatican before dawn

    Enter the Sistine Chapel with the key-keeper as he unlocks the doors — the room almost to yourself.

  2. 02

    Pasta with Nonna Rita

    Make fettuccine and tiramisù with our pastry chef's grandmother, then eat it in the garden.

  3. 03

    Vespa through the city

    A vintage Vespa and a local guide from the Aventine keyhole to the Gianicolo terrace at sunset.

  4. 04

    Margutta gallery walk

    Meet the painters and restorers still working on our street, with a private studio visit.

  5. 05

    Frascati wine day

    Lunch and tasting at a family vineyard in the hills, twenty-five minutes south of Rome.

  6. 06

    Courtyard hammam

    A small marble steam room and two treatment rooms using olive oil and Roman chamomile.

Good to know

Questions, answered

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 14:00 and check-out is until 11:00. Early arrival and late departure can be arranged on request and are guaranteed for direct bookings when the room is available.

How do I get to the hotel from the airport?

The nearest airport is Rome Fiumicino (FCO), around 40 minutes by private car. We can arrange a private transfer — simply tick the box when you send your reservation request.

What is your cancellation policy?

Flexible rates can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival. Advance-purchase rates are non-refundable but offer the lowest price.

Are children welcome?

Children of all ages are welcome. Cots are complimentary and extra beds can be added to most rooms and suites.

Do you allow pets?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome in the Garden Suites for €40 per night.

Is there parking?

Via Margutta is a limited-traffic zone. We arrange valet parking at a nearby garage for €45 per day — please tell us your plate number before arrival.
