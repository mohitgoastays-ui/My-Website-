Our story

Three generations of zardozi

Our grandfather was a zardozi master in Chandni Chowk who embroidered for royal families. In 1986 his son opened Zari House to bring that craft to brides across India — and today his granddaughter designs every collection.

Every lehenga takes 1,200 to 3,000 hours of handwork by our karigars. We use real zari, silk from Varanasi and colours inspired by Mughal miniatures — and we fit each piece to you, with three fittings included.

— Mehr Kapoor, Creative Director

Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but appointments guarantee a private stylist and fitting room.

How long do custom pieces take?

Bridal lehengas take 12–16 weeks; sarees and sherwanis 6–10 weeks. Express orders are possible for some designs.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — fully insured, tracked shipping worldwide, with duties shown before you pay.

What is your exchange policy?

Ready-to-wear pieces can be exchanged within 15 days. Custom pieces include free alterations.

Can I book a video consultation?

Yes — our stylists offer video appointments for clients abroad.
